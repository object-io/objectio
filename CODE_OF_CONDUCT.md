# Code of conduct

ObjectIO follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other place someone represents the project.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, give and take
criticism of the work rather than the person, and don't harass anyone.
Harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, and
publishing someone's private information are not accepted, whoever does
them.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code to the maintainers at
**ys@imys.in**. Reports are read only by the maintainers, kept
confidential, and answered. The maintainers decide what to do following
the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban.
